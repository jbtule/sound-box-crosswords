      Settings

      Home Screen

      Add Sound Box to the Home Screen to keep progress saved (Safari can clear it after a while unused) and play full screen, even offline.

      1. In Safari, tap Share (on iPhone it may be under •••).
      2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
      3. Open it from the new icon. Stars and pictures saved here don’t carry over; it starts fresh.

          Voice

          Pack words use a recorded voice. This device voice reads Custom words, and fills in if the recordings can’t load. For a more natural one on iPad or iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. Download one marked Enhanced or Premium, then reload this page. “Automatic” uses the best one on this device.

          Letter pad

          Custom words

          One word per line. Add dashes between syllables (rain-bow) so the colors and “Break it down” work. An emoji after the word becomes the picture; leave it off for words a picture can’t show, and the clue is “listen” only. Add a sentence in quotes to say the word in context: there "Put the box over there." If “Break it down” says a beat wrong, add how it sounds in brackets: pea-nut 🥜 (pee-nut). The starter list is an example — swap in this week’s school words.
