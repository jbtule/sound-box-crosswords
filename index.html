      Custom words

      One word per line. Add dashes between syllables (rain-bow) so the colors and “Break it down” work. An emoji after the word becomes the picture; leave it off for words a picture can’t show, and the clue is “listen” only. Add a sentence in quotes to say the word in context: there "Put the box over there." If “Break it down” says a beat wrong, add how it sounds in brackets: pea-nut 🥜 (pee-nut). The starter list is an example — swap in this week’s school words. Unlike the pack words, custom words aren’t recorded ahead of time: this device’s built-in voice reads them, so they can sound flatter or more robotic.
